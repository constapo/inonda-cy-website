AC service before summer: why it matters in Cyprus

Air conditioners in Cyprus work harder than almost anywhere in Europe. A spring service keeps them cool, quiet and cheaper to run.

4 min read · Last updated 2026-10-04

Why service in spring

In July and August technicians are fully booked and a breakdown means days without cooling. A service in March to May catches problems early, while dirty filters and coils are cleaned before the heavy season, when they would raise power use the most.

What a proper service includes

  • Deep cleaning of filters, coil and fan, with antibacterial treatment
  • Flushing the condensate drain to stop drips and smells
  • Cleaning the outdoor unit and straightening the fins
  • Checking refrigerant pressure, temperatures and electrical connections
  • Advice if a unit is too small or near the end of its life

Warning signs

  • Weak airflow or warm air
  • Water dripping from the indoor unit
  • Bad smell when the unit starts
  • Ice on pipes or the outdoor unit
  • Unusual noise or vibration

Rentals and holiday homes

Units in rentals and holiday homes are often used heavily by people who never clean the filters. A service before each season, or a maintenance plan, avoids complaints and emergency call-outs.

FAQ

Common questions

How often should I service my AC?

At least once a year; twice a year if you use it as heating in winter or in a rental.

Does servicing really reduce my bill?

Clean filters and coils let the unit cool with less effort, so it uses less electricity.
