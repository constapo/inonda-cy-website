Doors, windows & carpentry

Quieter, cooler and more secure: new windows and doors, plus fitted furniture made to measure.

Get a free quoteWhatsApp

Home transformation

What's included

We measure, supply and install windows, doors and built-ins, and repair what's worth keeping, from sliding door rollers to sagging wardrobe doors.

Windows & sliding doors

  • Aluminium thermal-break and PVC frames
  • Double and low-E glazing
  • Sliding, tilt-and-turn and lift-and-slide systems
  • Fly screens and roller shutters

Doors

  • Interior doors and frames
  • Security and entrance doors
  • Sliding pocket doors
  • Door adjustment, locks and hardware

Built-in furniture

  • Fitted wardrobes and walk-in closets
  • TV walls and storage units
  • Under-stair and alcove storage
  • Kitchen carpentry repairs

Repairs

  • Sliding door rollers and tracks
  • Window seals, handles and hinges
  • Shutter straps, motors and slats
  • Wooden door and frame restoration

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Thermal-break aluminium or PVC with double glazing can noticeably reduce AC costs and street noise.
  • Window replacement may qualify for energy-upgrade grants when combined with other works.

FAQ

Common questions

How long until new windows are installed?

Made-to-measure frames typically take a few weeks to manufacture; installation itself is usually one or two days per apartment.

Can you fix my sliding door instead of replacing it?

Often yes: new rollers, track repair and seals bring most sliding doors back to life.

Do you make wardrobes to measure?

Yes, designed for your space with your choice of finishes and interior layout.
