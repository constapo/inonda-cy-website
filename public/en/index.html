How we work

Five clear steps, from first call to handover

Every job, from a dripping tap to a full renovation, follows the same simple process, so you always know what happens next and what it costs.

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, WhatsApp or send the form. Photos and a short description help us prepare.

  2. Site visit & assessment

    We inspect, measure and find the cause, not just the symptom.

  3. Written, itemised quote

    Fixed scope, materials and price in writing. No work starts without your OK.

  4. Work done right

    Licensed technicians, agreed schedule, protected floors and furniture, daily clean-up.

  5. Handover & warranty

    We walk through the result with you, hand over certificates and the written warranty.

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Is the quote really free?

Assessment and written quote are free and without obligation for jobs estimated at €1,000 or more. For complex diagnostics we tell you any fee upfront. For small repairs estimated at less than €1,000, an on-site quotation visit costs €50 for properties outside Strovolos and up to 10 km away. Inside Strovolos, the quotation visit is free.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes. From one tap to a long list of small repairs. A minimum visit charge applies, so we suggest grouping jobs.

Who will actually do the work?

Our own team and licensed partner technicians who work to our standards, under our supervision and our warranty.

Which languages do you work in?

We answer, quote and work in Greek, English, Russian and Romanian. The website is also available in Ukrainian and Hebrew.
