Plumbing repairs & installations

From a dripping tap to a full re-pipe: licensed plumbers who find the real cause, explain the options and leave everything dry and clean.

Get a quoteWhatsApp

Repairs

What's included

Our plumbers handle hot and cold water, drainage and fittings in apartments, houses and commercial premises, for repairs as well as new installations during renovations.

Leaks & water damage

  • Leak detection in walls, floors and ceilings
  • Burst or corroded pipe repair and replacement
  • Leaking taps, valves, cisterns and flexible hoses
  • Shower and bath sealing, tray and waste leaks

Drains & blockages

  • Blocked sinks, showers, WCs and floor drains
  • Drain cleaning and odour problems
  • Soil stack and waste pipe repairs
  • Grease traps and outdoor manholes

Hot water & pressure

  • Solar water heater repair, cleaning and replacement
  • Electric water heaters and immersion elements
  • Pressure pumps, booster sets and pressure tanks
  • Roof water tanks, float valves and pipework

Installations

  • Complete hot and cold water pipework for renovations
  • Sanitaryware: WCs, basins, showers, bathtubs
  • Kitchen sinks, dishwasher and washing machine connections
  • Water filters and softeners against limescale

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Hard water across much of Cyprus causes limescale in heaters, taps and shower heads; a filter or softener pays for itself.
  • Know where your main stopcock is: we'll show you during any visit.
  • Low pressure on upper floors is often a pump, tank or air-lock issue, rarely the mains.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you find a leak without breaking the whole wall?

Yes. We use moisture meters, pressure testing and thermal imaging where useful, so we open only where the leak is.

Do you repair solar water heaters?

Yes: cleaning, element and thermostat replacement, leaking tanks or panels, and full replacement when repair isn't worth it.

Are your plumbers licensed?

Plumbing work is carried out by licensed, insured professionals working to Cyprus regulations.
