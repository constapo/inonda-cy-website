Larnaca

Local know-how

Larnaca's sea air and humidity are hard on outdoor AC units, metal railings and paint. Coastal apartments also see more condensation and mould in winter, so good ventilation and the right paints matter.

We plan Larnaca work in scheduled visits and run renovation projects with a dedicated crew, so jobs are grouped efficiently and finished on time.

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Salt air corrodes outdoor AC units and railings faster: plan yearly servicing.
  • Holiday rentals benefit from a pre-season maintenance visit.
  • Anti-mould paints and extractor fans help in coastal bathrooms.
