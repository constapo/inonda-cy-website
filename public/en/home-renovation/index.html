Home transformation

What's included

We plan the full scope with you, schedule every trade in the right order and keep you updated with photos, so the project stays on time and on budget.

Planning

  • Site survey, measurements and condition report
  • Scope, phasing and itemised budget
  • Material and finish selection support
  • Coordination with a licensed engineer or architect when permits are needed

Structure & services

  • Demolition and new internal layouts
  • Complete rewiring and new consumer unit
  • New plumbing, drainage and hot water
  • AC and heat pump pre-installation

Interiors

  • Gypsum board ceilings, partitions and lighting design
  • Plastering, rendering and painting
  • Floor tiling, laminate or engineered wood
  • Interior doors, wardrobes and kitchen

Exterior & efficiency

  • Roof waterproofing and insulation
  • New aluminium or PVC windows with double glazing
  • Facade repairs and painting
  • Balconies, terraces and outdoor areas

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Structural changes, extensions and some facade works need planning and building permits; we tell you early and coordinate with a licensed engineer.
  • Renovating a rental? We prioritise durable, easy-to-clean materials that cut future maintenance.
  • We share a weekly photo update, ideal for overseas owners.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a full apartment renovation take?

It depends on size and scope; a typical 2–3 bedroom apartment takes from several weeks to a few months. The quote includes a phased schedule.

Can you renovate while I'm abroad?

Yes. Many clients are overseas owners; we send regular photo and video updates and arrange approvals by WhatsApp or email.

How are payments structured?

In stages tied to completed phases, agreed in the written quote. You never pay far ahead of the work done.
