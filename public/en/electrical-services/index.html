Repairs

What's included

All electrical work is carried out by electricians licensed in Cyprus, with testing on completion and the certificates required for new or altered installations.

Faults & repairs

  • Tripping breakers and RCDs traced and fixed
  • Dead sockets, flickering lights, burning smells
  • Water-damaged circuits after leaks
  • Outdoor and garden lighting faults

Panels & safety

  • Consumer unit (fuse board) upgrades
  • RCD protection and surge protection
  • Earthing and bonding checks
  • Inspection and testing with written report

New installations

  • Full or partial rewiring for renovations
  • New sockets, switches, USB and data points
  • Dedicated lines for AC, ovens, hobs and water heaters
  • Three-phase upgrades for larger homes

Lighting & smart home

  • LED spotlights, gypsum ceiling and strip lighting
  • Outdoor, garden and security lighting
  • Smart switches, timers and sensors
  • EV charger installation

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Changes to an installation, and new connections or upgrades with the EAC (Electricity Authority of Cyprus), need certification by a licensed electrician.
  • Older apartments often lack RCD protection: an upgrade is quick and protects your family.
  • Planning a renovation? Rewire before plastering and flooring, never after.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you provide an electrical certificate?

Yes, for installations we carry out or inspect, our licensed electricians test and issue the required documentation.

My RCD keeps tripping, what does it mean?

A circuit or appliance is leaking current to earth, often moisture or a faulty appliance. We isolate circuits to find it.

Do you install EV chargers?

Yes, including checking your supply capacity and installing a dedicated protected circuit.
