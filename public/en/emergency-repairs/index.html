Repairs

What's included

First we make the property safe and stop the damage spreading. Then we give you a clear quote for the permanent repair.

Water

  • Burst and leaking pipes isolated and repaired
  • Leaks into the apartment below
  • Overflowing tanks, WCs and water heaters
  • Blocked drains backing up

Electrical

  • Total or partial power loss
  • Breakers that won't reset
  • Burning smell, sparking sockets
  • Water in light fittings

Security

  • Broken locks and doors that won't close
  • Forced entry repairs and boarding up
  • Broken glass made safe
  • Roller shutters stuck open or closed

Storm damage

  • Roof and terrace leaks during rain
  • Blocked rooftop drains and gutters
  • Fallen shutters, awnings and pergolas
  • Temporary protection until permanent repair

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Water leak? Close the main stopcock and switch off electricity to wet areas before we arrive.
  • Take photos and videos of the damage for your insurer before anything is moved.
  • We can provide a written report of cause and repair to support insurance claims.

How we work

Five clear steps, from first call to handover

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, WhatsApp or send the form. Photos and a short description help us prepare.

  2. Site visit & assessment

    We inspect, measure and find the cause, not just the symptom.

  3. Written, itemised quote

    Fixed scope, materials and price in writing. No work starts without your OK.

  4. Work done right

    Licensed technicians, agreed schedule, protected floors and furniture, daily clean-up.

  5. Handover & warranty

FAQ

Common questions

How fast can you come?

We prioritise urgent jobs and aim to attend the same day in Nicosia. Response depends on location and time of call; we always give you an honest ETA.

Do you charge more for urgent visits?

Out-of-hours and urgent call-outs may carry a call-out fee. We tell you before we come.

Can you help with my insurance claim?

Yes, we provide photos and a written report of the cause and the work carried out.
