Terms of Service

The basics of how quotes, works, payments and warranty work with us.

This website

Information on inonda.org is general and may change. Photos may be illustrative. Nothing on the site is a binding offer until we confirm a written quote.

Quotes

Quotes are written, itemised and valid for the period stated. Work starts only after you accept the quote. Additional work found during the job is quoted and agreed in writing before it's done.

Access & schedule

You provide access, water and electricity at the agreed times. Schedules may move for weather, material delivery or unforeseen conditions; we inform you promptly.

Payments

Small jobs are paid on completion. Projects follow the deposit and stage-payment plan in the quote. Materials remain our property until paid for.

Warranty

We provide a written warranty on our workmanship (minimum 6 months unless the quote states otherwise). Products carry their manufacturer's warranty. The warranty doesn't cover misuse, normal wear, or work altered by others.

Liability & law

We are insured for the works we carry out. These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Cyprus and the courts of Nicosia have jurisdiction.
