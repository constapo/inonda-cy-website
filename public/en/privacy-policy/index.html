Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to answer your request and carry out the work.

Who we are

INONDA LTD (registration HE 490415), 29 Charalampou Mouskou, 2014 Strovolos, Nicosia, Cyprus, is the controller of personal data collected through this website. Contact: inonda2026@outlook.com.

What we collect

Information you send us: name, phone, email, property area and type, and details or photos of the work needed.

Technical data needed to run the site (for example server logs). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

To reply to your request, prepare quotes, schedule and carry out work, issue invoices and provide warranty service.

Legal bases: steps at your request before a contract, performance of a contract, our legal obligations (such as accounting) and our legitimate interest in running our business.

Sharing

We share data only with the technicians and suppliers needed for your job, our accountants, and authorities where legally required. The quote form opens WhatsApp; messages you send there are also subject to WhatsApp's own terms.

Retention

Enquiries that don't become jobs are deleted within 12 months. Job and invoice records are kept as long as Cyprus tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data and object to processing by emailing us. You can also complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.
