Maintenance plans that prevent problems

A fixed schedule of checks and servicing for your home, rental, office or building, so faults are caught early, and when something does break, you're first in line.

Maintenance

What's included

We agree a checklist and calendar for your property, then visit on schedule, report what we find with photos and fix small issues on the spot.

Seasonal servicing

  • Air conditioner and heat pump cleaning before summer and winter
  • Solar water heater, boiler and immersion heater checks
  • Water pump, pressure tank and roof tank inspection
  • Gutter, drain and rooftop outlet clearing before the rains

Safety & systems

  • Electrical panel, RCD and earthing checks
  • Visible wiring, sockets and lighting inspection
  • Leak checks on taps, valves, WCs and under sinks
  • Smoke and CO detector testing and batteries

Building & finishes

  • Roof and terrace waterproofing inspection
  • Damp, mould and crack monitoring
  • Doors, windows, locks, hinges and shutters adjusted and lubricated
  • Silicone, grout and sealant renewal in wet areas

Reporting & priority

  • Photo report after every visit
  • Small repairs fixed during the visit
  • Priority call-outs for plan customers
  • One contact person who knows your property

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Rooftop water tanks and solar heaters work hard in the Cyprus heat and hard water causes limescale, so yearly checks extend their life.
  • Flat roofs should be checked in September–October, before the first heavy rains.
  • Landlords: a maintenance log helps with tenant disputes and deposit returns.

FAQ

Common questions

Who are maintenance plans for?

Homeowners, landlords with one or several rentals, offices, shops and residential buildings or committees that want common areas looked after.

How is the price set?

By property size, the equipment to service (for example number of AC units) and visit frequency. We inspect first and give you a fixed annual or monthly price.

Are repairs included?

Small fixes during the visit are included. Larger repairs are quoted separately and only done after your approval, at plan-customer rates.
