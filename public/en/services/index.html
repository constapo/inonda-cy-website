Maintenance

Planned care that keeps homes, rentals and businesses running, and stops small problems becoming expensive ones.

Maintenance plans

Scheduled servicing for homes, rentals, offices and buildings, with one point of contact and priority call-outs.

We agree a checklist and calendar for your property, then visit on schedule, report what we find with photos and fix small issues on the spot.

  • Air conditioner and heat pump cleaning before summer and winter
  • Solar water heater, boiler and immersion heater checks
  • Water pump, pressure tank and roof tank inspection
  • Gutter, drain and rooftop outlet clearing before the rains
  • Electrical panel, RCD and earthing checks

Air conditioning

Installation, servicing, deep cleaning, gas top-up and repair of split units, multi-splits and heat pumps.

Whether you need a new unit, a deep clean before July or a repair on a unit that's leaking or not cooling, we handle the whole job, including the electrics and the condensate drainage.

  • Help choosing the right size (BTU) and energy class for each room
  • Wall-mounted split, multi-split, ducted and cassette units
  • Neat pipe routing, trunking and core drilling
  • Dedicated electrical line and breaker where needed
  • Condensate drain to a proper outlet

Roof waterproofing & damp

Flat roof and terrace waterproofing, insulation, leak tracing and lasting fixes for damp and mould.

We first find where the water comes from: roof, terrace, pipes, condensation or rising damp. Then we recommend the right system and apply it on a properly prepared surface.

  • Inspection, moisture readings and photo report
  • Cleaning, crack repair and surface preparation
  • Elastomeric, cementitious, polyurethane or membrane systems
  • Reflective cool-roof coatings that cut heat gain
  • Detailing around tanks, solar heaters, drains and parapets

Plumbing

Leaks, blockages, water heaters, pumps, pipework and bathroom and kitchen fittings, done by licensed plumbers.

Our plumbers handle hot and cold water, drainage and fittings in apartments, houses and commercial premises, for repairs as well as new installations during renovations.

  • Burst or corroded pipe repair and replacement
  • Leaking taps, valves, cisterns and flexible hoses
  • Shower and bath sealing, tray and waste leaks
  • Blocked sinks, showers, WCs and floor drains
