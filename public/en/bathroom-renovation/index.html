Bathroom renovation, done properly underneath

A beautiful bathroom that stays watertight for years. We renew the pipes and waterproofing before a single tile goes on.

Get a quoteWhatsApp

Home transformation

What's included

From a quick refresh to a full strip-out, we handle everything: plumbing, electrics, waterproofing, tiling, sanitaryware, glass and lighting.

Strip-out & services

  • Removal of old tiles, sanitaryware and debris
  • New hot and cold water pipework and drains
  • Electrical points, extractor fan and lighting
  • Wall and floor levelling

Waterproofing

  • Tanking of shower areas and floors
  • Sealing tapes at corners, pipes and drains
  • Linear drains for level-access showers
  • Leak test before tiling

Tiling & fixtures

  • Large-format porcelain, mosaic and feature walls
  • Walk-in showers, glass screens, bathtubs
  • Wall-hung WCs with concealed cisterns
  • Vanity units, mirrors, storage niches

Comfort & finishing

  • Heated towel rails and mirror demisters
  • LED and mirror lighting
  • Accessible bathrooms: grab rails, level showers
  • Silicone, clean-up and handover

Good to know in Cyprus

  • Most bathroom leaks we repair come from missing waterproofing under the tiles. We never skip it.
  • A concealed WC cistern saves space and makes cleaning easier.
  • A typical full bathroom renovation takes around two to three weeks, depending on size and finishes.

How we work

Five clear steps, from first call to handover

  1. Tell us what you need

    Call, WhatsApp or send the form. Photos and a short description help us prepare.

  2. Site visit & assessment

    We inspect, measure and find the cause, not just the symptom.

  3. Written, itemised quote

    Fixed scope, materials and price in writing. No work starts without your OK.

  4. Work done right

    Licensed technicians, agreed schedule, protected floors and furniture, daily clean-up.

  5. Handover & warranty

    We walk through the result with you, hand over certificates and the written warranty.

FAQ

Common questions

Can you replace a bathtub with a walk-in shower?

Yes, one of our most common jobs, including drain changes, waterproofing and a glass screen.

Do I need to buy the tiles and fittings?

Either way: we can supply everything, or you choose from a showroom and we coordinate delivery.

Can you renovate a bathroom in a rental between tenants?

Yes. We plan the schedule tightly and can renovate in stages for multiple bathrooms.
