## 4. Photos (most important for views)

Google ranks profiles with **real, recent photos** much higher. Upload at least 10 at the start, then 2–3 every week:
- Logo (square) and a cover photo (a finished job or the team van).
- **Before / after** of real jobs: bathrooms, kitchens, roofs, painting.
- Team at work (Irina, Constantin, technicians), with tools and vans.
- Do **not** upload stock photos to Google Business Profile. Google may remove them and it hurts trust. The stock photos on the website are fine to use there.
- Upload the presentation video as well (max 75 MB, 30 s works best). Ask me for a 30-second cut.
